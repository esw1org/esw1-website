Tyler Lee Long, 1992 to 2009
Tyler's Story

He was a rule-follower who couldn't understand cruelty.

Tyler was high-functioning autistic, thoughtful and kind, and never understood cruelty. He died by bullycide on October 17, 2009, at 17. Since then, his mother Tina has fought to bring awareness to mental health and bullying.

"That your voice is a powerful tool, and you can change the world."

What We Stand For

Mission, vision, and goal

Mission

There's enough cruelty in the world. We bring the light, a resource for families who need someone in their corner, because everything starts with one.

Vision

That every child can learn in an environment free from bullying and harassment, and that mental health, including autism, is understood without stigma.
