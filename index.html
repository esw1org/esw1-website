Tyler's Story

He was a rule-follower who couldn't understand cruelty.

Tyler was high-functioning autistic, thoughtful and kind, and never understood cruelty. He died by bullycide on October 17, 2009, at 17. Since then, his mother Tina has fought to bring awareness and action to schools, families, and communities.

"That your voice is a powerful tool, and you can change the world."

What We Stand For

Mission, vision, and goal

Mission

To support children and the adults who care for them with honest, practical anti-bullying resources.

Vision

A world where every child feels seen, safe, and valued.

Goal

Give families, schools, and communities tools to intervene before cruelty escalates.

How ESW1 Helps

For anyone who comes in contact with a child touched by bullying

Everyone who supports a child needs a place to start.

For parents

Guidance, advocacy, and language for hard conversations.

For educators

Tools to notice warning signs and respond with care.

For students

Permission to speak up, ask for help, and support others.

The ESW1 Solution

A comprehensive approach, not a one-time assembly
